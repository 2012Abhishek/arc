ARC REACTOR

An HHO fuel cell that turns ocean water into clean fuel gas.

EXPLORE
THE PRODUCT

Water in. Fuel gas out.

The Arc Reactor is a dry-cell HHO generator. A stack of stainless steel plates splits water into hydrogen and oxygen on demand, producing a combustible gas mixture without burning anything to make it.

ALIGNED WITH UN SDG 7

Clean energy, within reach.

Sustainable Development Goal 7 calls for access to affordable, reliable, sustainable and modern energy for all. The Arc Reactor supports that aim by producing fuel gas from water, one of the most widely available resources on Earth, with no carbon emitted at the point of generation.

RESPONSIBLE WATER USE

Zero fresh water. Ocean only.

Water scarcity is a real concern, and we share it. The Arc Reactor is designed to run on saline ocean water, so it never competes with the fresh water people drink and farm with.
